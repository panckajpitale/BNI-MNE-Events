Optional: Live Auto-Sync to Google Sheets

If you'd like registrations submitted by attendees on their phones to instantly append as a new row in your central Google Sheet in real-time, copy this Google Apps Script into your Sheet:

function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([
    new Date(), 
    data.eventTitle, 
    data.fullName, 
    data.designation, 
    data.department, 
    data.homeChapter, 
    data.supportChapter, 
    data.transactionId, 
    data.phone, 
    data.email
  ]);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({"status":"success"})).setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}

When saved, every form submission on this site will also beam directly into your sheet.
